## What Tabush does

- **C1 compliance checks** per property or borrower (CAR, land titles, territories, embargoes).
- **C2 climate risk scores** for drought, flood and heat, per property and portfolio.
- **C3 parametric insurance indices** with backtests and automatic payout events.
