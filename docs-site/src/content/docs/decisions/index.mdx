A decision record captures a choice that is costly to reverse: the context,
the options, what we chose and why, and what would make us revisit it. The
format follows [MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/). Where it helps, a record
includes an interactive illustration, so a reader can test the decision
against their own priorities instead of taking it on trust.

## Template

```mdx
---
title: "NNNN: Short decision in plain words"
description: One sentence on what was decided.
---

import AdrMeta from "../../../components/AdrMeta.astro";

<AdrMeta status="proposed" date="YYYY-MM-DD" />

## Context
Who has the problem, what decision it supports, what constrains it.

## Options
## Decision
## Consequences
Good, bad, and what would make us revisit this.
```
